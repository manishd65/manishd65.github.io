About

Overview

I am a PhD candidate in the Vibro-Acoustics (FRITA) Laboratory at the Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru, working under the supervision of Prof. Venkata R. Sonti. My research focuses on weakly nonlinear acoustic wave propagation in rigid and flexible waveguides under mean flow, using analytical perturbation methods, frequency-domain numerical schemes, and finite element modelling.

Focus Areas

Research

My doctoral work addresses nonlinear acoustic wave propagation and sound-structure interaction in rigid and flexible waveguides carrying a mean flow, combining analytical perturbation methods with frequency-domain and finite-element-based numerical approaches.

Vibrations Acoustics Nonlinear Wave Propagation Sound-Structure Interaction Vibro-acoustics

Rigid Rectangular Waveguide

Developed analytical second order solutions for weakly nonlinear acoustic propagation in rigid rectangular waveguides with uniform mean flow. The work examines self-modal and cross-modal interactions, second harmonic generation, resonant and non-resonant modal coupling, multimodal nonlinear propagation, harmonic energy transfer, and progressive waveform distortion leading to acoustic shock formation.

Rigid Cylindrical Waveguide

Developed analytical and numerical formulations for weakly nonlinear acoustic propagation in rigid cylindrical waveguides containing a fluid with uniform mean flow. The work investigates nonlinear modal coupling, second harmonic generation, resonant and non-resonant interactions, spatial beats, and the influence of mean flow on multimodal wave propagation.

Flexible Rectangular and Cylindrical Waveguides

Studying nonlinear acoustic propagation and sound-structure interaction in flexible waveguides carrying a fluid with mean flow. Work on two-dimensional flexible rectangular guides couples the acoustic field and structural motion to investigate multimodal propagation, resonance and nonlinear modal interactions; extension to flexible cylindrical guides is ongoing.

Frequency-Domain Numerical Scheme (FDNS)

Developed a frequency-domain numerical framework for nonlinear multimodal acoustic propagation with mean flow. The scheme captures harmonic generation, modal energy transfer, resonant and non-resonant interactions, progressive waveform distortion, and nonlinear cascades leading toward acoustic shock formation.
